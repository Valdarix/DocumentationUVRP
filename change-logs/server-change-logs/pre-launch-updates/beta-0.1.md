# Beta 0.1

### Overview

With this update I consider the city playable but not perfect. Things need tweaked, fixed, added, tuned, etc. But it is playable so with that we move from Alpha Notes to Beta Notes, my hope is actual Relases version 1.0 on November 1st. That doesn't mean we have to wait until November 1st to actually get in and RP that is just when we will open to the public and start taking applications.&#x20;

{% hint style="danger" %}
If you have close friends, that you trust and want to invite before November 1st, now is the time to do that. Starting November 1st everyone regardless of who they are and who they know will be required to fill out the application and be approved. We will be very picky about our application acceptance starting on that date. They can join by going to [discord.pointhope.xyz](https://discord.pointhope.xyz) once they have joined anyone with staff perms should be able to give them member role.&#x20;
{% endhint %}
