### Out Of City

* Added new Who We Are page to the player guide

### General Updates:

* Several items have had their images fixed and several broken shops have been fixed to have correct stocks.
* USB chargers should now work

### Crime/CivJobs/Activities

* Added new trucking script. You can now Convoy with other players and level up your trucking skills. Once unlocked up to 8 players can engage in a convoy. Available on laptops purchased at Digi Den
* New Chop script added, work as a team to level your boosting and chopping skills, work as a team of up to 4. Chop tablet is sold at an electronics blackmarket in town.&#x20;
* Crypto has been tuned to be more in line with economy goals
* You can now farm more items, with more coming soon! Custom props for several items.&#x20;

### Whitelist Jobs:

* Fixed several issues relating to Black Lantern and Cafe Day setup. These jobs should be good to go now. There are still some missing ingredients from the options that will be added soon.&#x20;
