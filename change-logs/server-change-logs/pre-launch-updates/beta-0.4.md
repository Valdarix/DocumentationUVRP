### Drugs (Meth/Coke/Weed)

#### All:

* These drugs have a purity system. Each player has a different recipe to achieve perfect purity so no one is able to give away their own perfect recipe to others. The better quality your drugs are of this type the more they sell for.
* When processing the higher your level the more you yield! Warehouses will yield more than tables.
* Players can have only 1 of each type of table but can use another players table.
* Warehouses for each type of drug have had their required levels updated as well as the number of stashes available in each warehouse.
* There is a 1% chance that police can be notified by an anonymous caller about strange odors in the area.
* Police can raid warehouses! But must stakeout and obtain a search warrant before they can raid (Process will be determined)
* Consumption the big 3 drugs can cause you to OD!

#### Meth

* All blips have been removed and the peds have moved to their final locations.
* Methhead Mark who will give you a table has relocated and stop broadcasting where he lives.
* Warehouse seller has relocated and stopped broadcasting his location. Catch him on break to buy a warehouse.
* Supply run guy has moved to his final location and his blip hidden, he can be found hanging out with Methhead Mark
* Players are limited to 50 supply runs per server restart
* Supply crates give a randomized amount of items per open.

#### Weed

* Players can grow 20 plants at a time.
* Players can farm weed out in the world to get seeds and get started processing.
* Requires a pot which has 70% chance to be returned on harvest and a shovel.
* Supply shop Smoke on the Water sells supplies from an NPC, this blip is not hidden (when one of the three weed shops in city opens these items will be moved to one of those shops)
* Currently one place exists on the map to farm weed plants. More will be added.

#### Coke

* Leaves farming field has moved. While farming leaves you have a 5% chance to get your own seeds for growing! (I apologize in advance)
* Growing your own requires a pot and you can grow up to 10 plants. Monitor your plants and be sure to take care of them or they will reduce your yield or even die!
* Personally grown plants have a higher yield potential if taken care of.
* Limited to 50 cement gathers per server restart but now yields more than 1 per gather
* Find Nervous Ron to purchase supplies such as shears, water can, fertilizer, planting pot, baking soda, empty baggy, gasoline or a coke table.
* Nigel (Lab Seller) can be found seeking knowledge
