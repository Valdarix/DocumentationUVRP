## BANKING

Banking script has been updated to the newest version of the script bringing with it new features:

### Credit Scores and Loans

* Manage your credit scores and take out loans at the bank.&#x20;
* Must have 10% of the loan amount in your bank account to secure the loan.&#x20;
* Interest rate depends on your credit score so don't be late on those payments.
* Account must be 10 days old to qualify for a loan

### Savings Accounts

* Add money to a savings account to generate interest

## BILLING

Billing system updated to the newest version bringing with it new features:&#x20;

* Quick invoice can be accessed with `F9` so you don't have to have the full menu.&#x20;
* New UI colors.

### Player to Company Invoices

* Players can now invoice a company instead of a person

### Company to Company Invoices

* Companies can invoice other companies for goods and services!
