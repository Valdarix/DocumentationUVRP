# Release 1.5

### General

* New Skills (Hacking, Lockpicking, Electrical, Safe Cracking and Explosives) level these to make criminal life easier.

### Civ Jobs/Activities

* Trucking should now spawn with full tanks and the fuel consumption should be lowered. (Working with trucking script dev to figure out why you can't actually fuel up the truck)

### Crime

* Drug selling can now be done anywhere using /corner, the radial menu and 3rd eye. Scales with police count.
* Heist overhaul - I got tired of the annoying base stuff. Custom heists for: \
  \-- Sandy Bank (Requires 2 people to start it) \
  \-- Fleecas (Requires a good hacker \
  \-- Paleto \
  Pacific Bank Progression is Fleeca > Paleto > Sandy > Pacific \
  \-- Hunting store - hit the hunting store for rare but good rewards \
  \-- Vangelico - Grab a bat, crowbar, or hammer to smash cases after you've disabled the doors \
  \-- ATMs (requires a hacking device from hackers den and a BankCard also from hackers den). Interior ATMS will be added at a later date. \
  \-- Store Robbery (Gas stations, convivence stores, Rob's liquor) - Might have missed a few.\
  Note: Pacific Bank is not completed.\
  This sets up the ability to add quite a few new heists (Human Labs, Yacht Heist and a few others are on my radar)
* Hackers Den has limited supply of stocks for heists and robberies. These items will be craftable as well soon
* Skills dictate the difficulty of a heist the more you do something the better you will be at it.
