# FAQ

### Can we add a car to the city?

> #### In most cases the answer to this question is always going to be 'No'. Most of the time when players are asking to add vehicles to the city that are wanting to add name brand cars like Lambos, BMWs, Mustangs, etc. In order to keep our city on the right side of Rockstar enforcement we will not add any name brand item to the city, even if it is "debadged". However you can open a ticket and discuss the possibility.&#x20;

{% hint style="info" %}
The most recent PLA from FIveM/Rockstar can be found here [https://runtime.fivem.net/platform-license-agreement-12-sept-2023.pdf](https://runtime.fivem.net/platform-license-agreement-12-sept-2023.pdf)\
\
_(ii) importation or misuse of other IP in the project, including other Rockstar IP, real-world brands, characters, trademarks or music._
{% endhint %}

### But you have music on the load screen.

> Our music is created using Suno.ai using a paid account with commercial rights by our owner. This allows us to create and use decent custom music for our load screens without breaking TOS.&#x20;

### Can I donate a script to the city?

> Yes. However you must open a ticket with your request and it will be discussed with the owner as well as any terms of that donation if approved.&#x20;

### Why don't you use LVC for sirens?&#x20;

> I get it, people love LVC, it feels realistic and cool, but the script sucks. It hasn't been updated in over 3 years and causing servers to lag. Most players don't even use all the extra features LVC provides. We use a fleet of TOS emergency vehicles that come with their own siren script, the script is performant and well tested and we have no desire to change it.
