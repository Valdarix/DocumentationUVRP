# Staff Meetings

{% hint style="warning" %}
In the interest of transparency monthly staff meetings are broadcast live for all to listen to.
{% endhint %}

Regularly scheduled all staff meetings will be held to make sure staff is always on the same page. These meetings will have a strict agenda that is posted ahead of the meeting, so everyone has time to prepare. These will occur every 2-4 weeks, never to exceed a period of 4 weeks between meetings.&#x20;

The meetings will take place in Discord and will be streamed live in Discord for any community members who wish to listen in. Staff exists to enhance and grow the community, and transparency goes a long way to building player trust.&#x20;
