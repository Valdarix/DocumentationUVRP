# Purpose

The purpose of this guide is to provide you with not only the expectations of your role but also provide you with information on how to use any tools that you may need to do your job effectively.&#x20;

As a staff member you should be familiar with the PHRP[ Rule Book](https://cornerstonedev.gitbook.io/diversionrp/getting-started/quickstart) found in the [RP Player Guide](https://cornerstonedev.gitbook.io/phrp). Both of these resources are available publicly and should be utilized in your day-to-day activities as a staff member to ensure consistency among staff members. These resources are kept up to date to make sure everyone has the most current and accurate information.&#x20;

If you notice anything missing, incomplete or outdated please open a tech support ticket in the Discord to have it addressed.&#x20;
