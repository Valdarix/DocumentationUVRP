# Staff Misconduct

{% hint style="danger" %}
Staff misconduct is not tolerated. As has been stated before bad staff kills cities.&#x20;
{% endhint %}

### Before you take the ticket:

Do not handle the ticket if you are not a Sr Admin or above

If you are acquainted with the party accused, then do not handle the report.&#x20;

If you do not feel you can be objective and fair, then do not handle the ticket.&#x20;

***

Staff Misconduct Includes:

* Abuse of in city admin powers in anyway.&#x20;
* Constantly using your roleplay character to do staff duties.&#x20;
* Providing information, you know only because you are staff to your friends to gain an advantage personally or for your friend group.
* Handling similar tickets in extremely different ways due who was involved.
* Representing DRP in any way that is against the [Etiquette ](../../../etiquette.md)discussed in the previous section.
