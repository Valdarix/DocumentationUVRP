# Trolling Discord

Anyone who is found to be trolling the Discord in anyway should be pulled into a ticket and talked to. If it is made clear what they did was not OK and that they should stop and they troll again then they should be removed from the Discord immediately. You MUST cite both this staff guideline and the rule from the rule book in the ticket where you remove them. \
\
They must also be Muted for 7 days using the \`/mute\` command in Discord. \
<br>

{% hint style="danger" %}
TROLLING - Any action that is meant to cause disruption or harm to members of the discord community. Or any action that is meant to tarnish the reputation of Diversion RP.&#x20;
{% endhint %}
