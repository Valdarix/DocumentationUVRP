# Moderation and Spectating

{% hint style="info" %}
As always remember to be on a staff character when moderating or spectating.&#x20;
{% endhint %}

In-city moderation and spectating are expected of both Jr. Admins and Admins. Trial Admins do not have in city admin permissions.&#x20;

The goal of moderation and spectating is NOT to catch players and put them in tickets for breaking rules. Unless a player is egregiously breaking rules or exploiting, we should treat every infraction as a learning opportunity.&#x20;

Some things can be addressed without even talking direclty to the player. They should only be pulled into a ticket if it is believed what they did needs to be addressed only to them. Other infractions can be addressed with Discord announcements to educate everyone.

What you should not do unless absolutely necessary:

* Freeze a player
* Teleport a player
* Interrupt a scene
* End a scene
* Reveal your presence
* Place a player in a ticket
