# Specific Staff Duties

{% hint style="info" %}
Remember any in city moderation should be handled by someone on a staff character.&#x20;
{% endhint %}

### General Duties:&#x20;

* Guiding the city's direction, aligning with the vision set by the Senior Staff.
* Help shape new roleplay avenues to enhance the city.&#x20;
* Provide examples of great roleplay at all times.&#x20;
* Engage in conversations with newcomers and members alike to help everyone feel included and welcome.
* Collaborate within our staff teams not only with each other but with other teams to help get the answer a member might need.
* Avoid interrupting scenes unless there is a serious offense requiring immediate removal from the city, which must be approved by a Senior Administrator. RP coaching should happen within the ticket system or via Discord VCs, not in city.&#x20;
* Assist liaisons in tracking the things that need to be tracked.&#x20;

### Discord Duties:

* Acknowledge tickets promptly and maintain active communication about the status of the ticket.&#x20;
* Ensure timely acceptance of applications, aiming to integrate new players into the city within 24 hours of their acceptance.
* Maintain a positive attitude when dealing with members who have answered questions incorrectly and give them ample time to understand and correct their answers.&#x20;
* Possess thorough knowledge of PHRP Rules and be competent in discussing and enforcing these rules among the player base.
* Promptly add all disciplinary actions to the discipline tracker.&#x20;

### Media Duties:

* Ensure that streams accurately represent the city's roleplay, showcasing it as intended by the city's vision and standards.
* Help moderate and maintain a positive and engaging atmosphere in stream chats, fostering a welcoming and enjoyable community environment.
* Verify that streams adhere to the guidelines established by PHRP.
* Offer assistance to streamers with setup, including hardware recommendations and technical advice, to optimize their streaming quality and performance.
