# ℹ️ Whitelist Appication

Every person interested in joining Point Hope RP must complete a whitelist application. Our application process is long and we do scrutinize every application that comes in with great detail. We are very selective in our approval process and we seek only those players whose ideals align with our servers vision.

The questions in the application should be answered with care.&#x20;

You should read the Rules and the Roleplay Guide before you start your application and should refer back to those pages if you are unsure about your answer.&#x20;
