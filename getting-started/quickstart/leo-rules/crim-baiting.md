# Crim-baiting

## **Police Entrapment & Artificial Crime Creation – Not Allowed**

### **The Rule:**

Law enforcement officers are **prohibited from artificially creating opportunities to entice criminals into committing crimes** or manufacturing arrest opportunities through aggressive, unrealistic policing tactics.

Police roleplay should focus on **responding to organic criminal activity, conducting realistic investigations, and allowing the criminal ecosystem to develop naturally.**

***

### **What Is NOT Allowed:**

❌ **Location Camping Without Justification:**

* Sitting at known drug locations, chop shops, or criminal hotspots without investigative buildup or active calls
* Staking out areas 24/7 waiting for criminals to show up
* Repeatedly patrolling the same criminal locations hoping to catch someone

❌ **Fishing For Minor Violations To Escalate:**

* Aggressively looking for tiny infractions (broken taillight, slightly crossing the line, etc.) specifically to create probable cause where none exists
* Using minor traffic violations as pretexts to search/harass specific individuals repeatedly
* Escalating minor stops into major investigations without legitimate reason

❌ **Following Suspicious Individuals Until They "Slip Up":**

* Tailing people without probable cause, waiting for them to make a mistake
* Harassing known criminals by following them constantly hoping they'll commit a crime
* Creating pressure through presence to force someone into doing something arrestable

❌ **Manufacturing Arrest Opportunities:**

* Setting up scenarios designed to trick or entice people into committing crimes
* Creating situations where criminals have no choice but to break the law
* Using bait tactics that wouldn't exist in realistic policing

***

### **What IS Allowed & Encouraged:**

✅ **Responding to organic criminal activity:**

* Dispatched calls and 911 reports
* Crimes you witness naturally while on patrol
* Tips from citizens or informants

✅ **Realistic investigations with buildup:**

* Gathering evidence over time on a suspect or organization
* Conducting surveillance **after establishing probable cause** through investigation
* Building cases through detective work, warrants, and proper procedure

✅ **Community engagement and patrol:**

* Regular patrols through varied areas of the city
* Traffic stops for legitimate, observable violations
* Building relationships with civilians and business owners

✅ **Allowing criminals space to operate:**

* Giving criminal organizations room to develop their storylines
* Not shutting down every crime immediately
* Understanding that a thriving criminal underworld creates better RP for everyone

***

### **Why This Rule Exists:**

**1. Realism:** Real police departments respond to crimes and investigate based on evidence – they don't camp drug corners 24/7 or manufacture reasons to arrest people.

**2. Balance:** If police are constantly pressuring criminal locations and players, criminals can't develop storylines, build organizations, or create the conflict that makes the server interesting.

**3. Better Stories:** Investigations that build over time, with evidence gathering and dramatic arrests, create far better RP than camping a spot and arresting everyone who shows up.

**4. Fairness:** Criminals should face consequences for their actions, but they also need space to operate and create stories. Artificial pressure ruins the criminal RP experience.

**5. Community Health:** A server where criminals can't function because police are manufacturing arrests constantly becomes stale and drives away half the playerbase.

***

### **What Good Police RP Looks Like:**

✅ **Investigation-driven:** Building cases over days/weeks, gathering evidence, coordinating with detectives\
✅ **Responsive:** Answering calls, patrolling diverse areas, reacting to crimes organically\
✅ **Community-focused:** Building relationships, knowing regular citizens, being visible but not oppressive\
✅ **Story-oriented:** Understanding that catching the criminal is one chapter in a longer story\
✅ **Balanced:** Giving criminals room to breathe while still providing realistic law enforcement presence

***

### **The Balance:**

Police should be **effective but not omnipresent, proactive but not predatory.**

**Yes, investigate crimes.** Build cases, follow leads, conduct stakeouts when you have cause.

**No, don't camp criminal areas 24/7.** Let the criminal ecosystem breathe and develop.

**Yes, make traffic stops for legitimate violations.** Enforce the law realistically.

**No, don't fish for reasons to search and harass.** Don't manufacture probable cause.

**The goal:** Create a realistic law enforcement presence that challenges criminals appropriately without suffocating criminal RP entirely.

***

### **Consequences:**

Violating this rule will result in:

* **Removal from the police department** (first or repeated offense depending on severity)
* **Potential disciplinary action** on your civilian characters as well
* **Coaching and retraining** for minor first-time offenses
* **Permanent LEO blacklist** for egregious or repeated violations

**Command staff and administration will monitor police activity** for patterns of entrapment, camping, or manufactured arrests.

***

### **The Bottom Line:**

**Police exist to respond to crime, not create it.**

Be the investigators, the responders, the community protectors. Don't be the predators camping criminal spawns waiting to farm arrests.

**Criminals need space to operate so you have crimes to investigate.** Give them room to build their empires – then take them down through good detective work and realistic policing.

**Better investigations = better stories for everyone.**

_Questions about whether a specific policing tactic crosses the line? Consult with your command staff or open a ticket with administration._
