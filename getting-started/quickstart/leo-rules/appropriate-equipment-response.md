# Appropriate Equipment/Response

You should follow SOPs at all times. The kit you have on you should only ever include what you need for that shift based on your rank and duties. \
\
You should not deploy SWAT equipment, vehicles, etc. without approval from high command.&#x20;

You should respond to situations appropriately. Call for back up when needed. Cops are not a gang and should not behave like one.&#x20;
