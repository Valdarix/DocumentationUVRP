# Scene Abandonment

It is never appropriate to end a scene you consider unimportant or low profile just to go to something that is a higher priority. For example if you are on a traffic stop, mid scene and a robbery ping hits you should not instantly end the traffic stop to head to the robbery. This is unfair to the person you are already engaged with.

The only time this is acceptable is in the instance of an All Call or Panic Button. However you should still end the scene appropriately `This is you lucky day` or something else to end the RP naturally.
