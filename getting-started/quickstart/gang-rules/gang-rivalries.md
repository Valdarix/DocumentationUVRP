# Gang Rivalries

* Gang Rivalries are not war.,
* Simple disrespect between gangs must NOT lead to an escalation higher than disrespect (trash-talk) without prior escalation. (i.e you cannot go straight to shooting.)
* Rivalries must be had before war is started.
* You must always RP before starting a rivalry. Rivalries cannot be initiated via drive-bys in opposing gang territories, in fact a drive by should not be used in the early phases of a rivalry.
* You are subject to police interactions, you cannot make police leave and you must fear RP if police arrive and scatter.
* Simple things like kidnappings, stabbings etc, fall under the rivalry category.
* If a scene ends with a shooting there must be 30 minutes from the time the last person is revived before you enter their territory again to start another scene or initiate another conflict.
* See the [Gang System](https://cornerstonedev.gitbook.io/phrp/gangs/gang-system) section of this guide for more information!

{% hint style="danger" %}
Your rival should be a long term rival and there should be lots of roleplay to support the rivalry. You should never go from Rivalry to Friends over a short period of time. They are your rival you should not be constantly hanging around with them and should avoid them.&#x20;

This is not a server where gangs have a different rival every other week and where a war is requested all the time. Wars are serious and have serious implications, you are building the lore of your gang and characters and you should strive for top notch story telling.

You DO NOT have to have a rival or beef with other gangs, but if you pushed you should respond accordingly. &#x20;
{% endhint %}
