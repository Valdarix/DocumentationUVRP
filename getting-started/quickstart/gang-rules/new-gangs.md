# New Gangs

### Who is this gang council?

The gang council is made up of all of the gang leaders of **recognized** gangs in the city. They control all gang activity in the city, and it is your job to prove you should be among their ranks.

More details of the gang councils' responsibilities are included later in these rules.

### Unrecognized Status

All new gangs will be considered unrecognized upstart gangs and must have approval to run as an upstart gang from one member of the gang council. Upstart gangs are allowed to do all gang-related activities except gang wars and gang beef. Upstart gangs should be focused on proving that they deserve to be a recognized gang.

You should seek out a gang that most aligns with your gangs focus or might be more likely to approve you. For example if you want to start a street gang on Davis, seeking out the gang on Grove Street might not be wise, but you might have better luck with an MC who is rivaled with the Grove Street crew.&#x20;

### Naming Your Gang

You may not under any circumstances use the name of a real-life gang, hate group, or criminal organization. This is against the FiveM/Rockstar PLA/TOS

These include but are not limited to: MS13, Crips, Bloods, KKK, Dixie Mafia, etc.

### Recognized Status

Becoming a recognized gang in the city is a big deal, but it also is not easy. Our city does not force you to create applications in discord with long backstories and documentation. Everything is done in city and is organic. Earning a recognized spot is a big deal and a big undertaking.

So how do you become a recognized gang in the city?

1. You already completed the first step by getting approval to form an upstart from the gang council.
2. During your two-week warm up period how, your gang operates is being closely monitored by the gang council. Every action you take is a mark in the ledger, so act wisely. Make yourself known to all gangs not just your sponsor gang.
3. After your two-week warm up period you may once again approach the council and ask for consideration to become recognized. Make sure you are ready!
4. You will need to provide each member gang of the council with a specific amount of money. So, make sure your finances are in order.
5. If your gang has proven yourself to that council member they can call a council meeting to session, you will NOT attend this meeting. At the meeting the sponsoring council member will state your case to the rest of the council and the Overseer.&#x20;
6. If you are approved by a vote of the council then you will be summoned to the meet the Overseer and receive your token.
7. With your token you will be show to Franklin, who handles the Overseer's day to day operations, where you will register you gang to become a recognized gang.&#x20;

If you are denied the right to become an official gang you will be told why you were denied and if you can overcome the denial.

{% hint style="danger" %}
When voting for gangs to become official the gang council members must act within the confines of roleplay and vote in their best interest.&#x20;

The sponsoring gang must vote YES, as they felt the gang had proven itself and called the council.

If done right the balance of gangs should be organic.
{% endhint %}
