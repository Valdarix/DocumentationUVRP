# Recognized Gang Benefits

### **Recognized Organizations can**:

* Use any gang MLO compound already in city free of charge.

You can purchase other options from our Tebex store, but these are 100% optional and are in no way required to be a gang.

* Purchase a custom MLO/Compound to be placed on your turf. (Request in a ticket to confirm the MLO and price which is subject to change) (if open),
* Add custom sprays and clothing(if slots open).,
* MLO’s, EUP and Sprays must first go through approval in a ticket in Discord, via your organization’s leadership.,

All benefits are subject to removal if recognized status is revoked.

* Going under the 5-member minimum will revoke your recognized status., Note that this is not an instant thing. Your leadership will be called by the rest of the gang council and given a two-week warning to get you numbers and activity back up before it is remove. Removal will be done at a gang council meeting with the Overseer present.

Organizations are allowed to own businesses.

* Must comply with all business rules and representation of your organization on the business property. Review business rules for more information on this.
* Your gang must operate the business properly; it cannot be used as a passive income farm.
* You can perform illegal actions out of your business but this exposes your business to being revoked if caught.&#x20;

Recognized have access to the gang menu and can form alliances, engage in rivalries, beef and war.&#x20;
