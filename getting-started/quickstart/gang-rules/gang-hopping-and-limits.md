# Gang Hopping and Limits

There are no limits to how many gangs a player can join or how often a gang member can change gangs. It is up to you as the player to protect your reputation and show your loyalty. Lots of gang hopping could prevent future gangs from taking you in.&#x20;

If a gang leader wants to accept someone who has been in 4 gangs in three weeks, that is their risk to take. It is up the gang leaders and their leadership to vet recruits and determine if they are worthy to join.&#x20;
