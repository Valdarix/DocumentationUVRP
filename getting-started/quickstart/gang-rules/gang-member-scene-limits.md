# Gang Member Scene Limits

**Unrecognized Gang**

* 5 members allowed for gang on gang interactions
* 4 members +1 lookout for criminal interactions

**Recognized Gangs**

* 7 Members for gang on gang interactions,
* 6 members + 1 lookout for criminal interactions,
* If you are pushed on your **BLOCK** you can use all your members. This **does not** include any territory you have taken over, it must be the zone you registered with Franklin. Other territories you own can be defended by 7 members as mentioned above.&#x20;
* Members that are not verified on your roster in the gang menu **CANNOT** flag your gang or be included in gang activity as a member.

{% hint style="success" %}
The LEO +2 rule applies to all interactions.
{% endhint %}
