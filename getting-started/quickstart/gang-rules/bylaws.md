# Bylaws

Your gang or group is required to have bylaws that dictate to players how your gang works. Your blood in-blood out process, recruiting process, rank structure, etc. This must be posted in a Gang Discord or an available google document.&#x20;

You must also submit your bylaws to staff in a ticket so we can make sure that the bylaws are available in DIscord and this guide.

If your bylaws have a perma death provision it must layout the circumstances that will result in a perma for the person. Perma's can not be a broad thing, it must be a very detailed set of reasons you might request a perma.&#x20;

All perma requests must be supported by evidence and will be reviewed by other members of the gang council and approved or denied by the council.&#x20;
