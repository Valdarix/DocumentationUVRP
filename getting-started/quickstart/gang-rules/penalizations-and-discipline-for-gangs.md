# Penalizations & Discipline for Gangs

* \
  Gang punishment is determined by the Overseer and the gang council. Any penalties, fines and punishments are determined by the council members not involved is handed down during a gang council meeting.
* We also utilize our Gang Menu system to provide punishment by timing a gang out from certain activities for a period of time.
* Any specific guidelines and restrictions your gang is given will be provided to you at the time of the gang council meeting. These must be followed or face further punishments.
