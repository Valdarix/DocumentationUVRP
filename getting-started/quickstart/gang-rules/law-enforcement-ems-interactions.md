# Law Enforcement / EMS Interactions

Violence must be the final step when interacting with cops that inject into gang on gang activity

* Police must not be interfered with while conducting police business (i.e.. traffic stops) unless the situation involves your gang as a whole and it makes sense to third party as explained in the the [Third Party](../crime-rules/third-partying.md) rule
* Law enforcement will be trained to not shoot on sight, the same rules must be followed by the gangs as well
* **You cannot make police leave and you must fear RP if police arrive and scatter if you are in the middle of a criminal activity that is not a heist/robbery scene.**&#x20;
* EMS will not be a gang's personal revive bot. Should your character be injured or go down in a gang-on-gang situation, EMS will not come on the scene until the end of the RP. If the RP is in a red zone (trap houses, drug processing, etc.), EMS may not respond.
