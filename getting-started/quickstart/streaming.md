# Streaming

Anyone in the PHRP community can stream their roleplay. There is no application process or gate keeping. All that we ask is that you open a ticket and let us know you are going to be streaming so that we can:

* Give you the PHRP Streamer Role in Discord
* Add you to the Live Now bot
* and so, our media team has your stream link so they can stop by from time to time.

It is important to remember that streamers must follow all of the same rules as the rest of the server and will not be given special permissions, perks or leniency just to suit your content. \
\
We do not moderate your streams or dictate your content, we do ask that you represent PHRP in a positive way and if you do not we may ask you to no longer stream the city or to make reasonable adjustments but we do reserve the right to remove you from the community if you continue to stream without making requested adjustments.&#x20;

A Few things not OK on stream

* Showing restricted or hidden locations (Drug fields, processing, money wash, black market, other hard to find locations)
* Bashing other communities
* Bashing PHRP Staff or discussing tickets
* Complaining about other peoples RP
* Complaining about prison sentences or how long a scene is taking

At the end of the day we want everyone to be able to stream and make content, but in a positive way that helps grow PHRP. Your stream should be focused on the roleplay and overall story arc of your character and should be focused on content meant for short form views.
