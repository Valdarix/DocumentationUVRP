# VIP and Tebex

### Tebex Shop

These are 100% optional, you do not need to spend money on this server for us to keep it alive. It is appreciated if you do, and we can't express how much we appreciate it. The things you can buy on the Tebex store are meant to be purely cosmetic or provide some small benefits to you. But should never be considered Pay to Win, anything that is in the shop that is considered to overpowered or PTW will be balanced. In no way will you ever need a Tebex purchase to enjoy this city.

Some things a priced kind of high, this isn't to gouge for money it is to&#x20;

* Make sure we can afford to add what you are requesting
* To prevent everyone from coming into the city and buying up a bunch of stuff we can't use or don't need.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Due to Tebex rules there are some things listed that are not on the shop and require us to create individual special packages. These packages are listed below with a special package flag. If you want to purchase a special package you must open a ticket, get the package/request approved and allow time for the package to be created. Payment for special packages is done through Tebex like any other package and must be TOS compliant.&#x20;
{% endhint %}

What are the VIP Tiers

* Gold VIP
* Silver VIP

### Selling Your Benefits

If you want to sell or give away your VIP benefits go for it, yes, I know people will abuse this, but who cares. VIP exists for people who want to monetarily support the server, not hide things from all of the other players. I assume if you bought VIP it is because you want to help keep us online. Not everyone can do that. So thank you deeply for even the smallest assistance.

What can you sell:

* Cars or Bikes purchased from VIP dealerships to your friends
* Your MLO house (note that you can only purchase 1 MLO house as a Gold VIP so if you sell it to someone you can not buy a second one as their MLO house still counts toward your limit)
