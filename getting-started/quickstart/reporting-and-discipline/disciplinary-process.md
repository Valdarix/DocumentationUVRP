# Disciplinary Process

Philosophy At Point Hope RP we do not believe in giving people goofy in city punishments like "coms" or "staff jail". We also believe that a rigid discipline process does not provide for a fair assessment of all situations. With this in mind our [Point Hope Staff Guide](https://cornerstonedev.gitbook.io/staffguide) is available to everyone to review how staff will handle specific situations. We hope that this transparency helps players and staff know exactly how a situation is going to be handled.

We believe in a teach, correct, remove process that is applied to all members of the community equally. The same way a ticket is handled for a member is the same way it would be handled for a senior staff member.&#x20;

If you do get removed from the community, you will be told why and you will be given a time frame for reapplication if applicable.
