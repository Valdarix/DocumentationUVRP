# Restaurant Script & Customizations

## **Restaurant System**

This guide covers the enhanced script used across all PHRP restaurants.&#x20;

***

### **System Overview**

**Key Features:**

* Owner and manager-controlled operations
* Customizable recipes and menus
* Inventory and stash management
* Employee duty system
* Music and ambiance controls
* Optional NPC deliveries for slow times
* Offline Ped

**Boss Menu Access:**

* Currently limited to **Owner rank only** (can be adjusted per business if needed)

### **Recipe System**

**Recipe Limits:**

* **Up to 8 ingredients** per recipe
* **25 total recipes** per business (this limit can be adjusted by staff if needed)
* **Maximum price per item:** $1,000 (adjustable if necessary)

**Nutritional Values:**

* Final recipe nutritional value is determined by the ingredients used
* **Food ingredients** provide hunger restoration
* **Drink ingredients** provide thirst restoration

_Note: Buffs (special effects from food/drinks) are currently disabled until staff determines ingredient distribution per restaurant type._
