# Ingredients

### Overview

We offer a lot of ingredients for your restaurant recipes. Some players like detailed intricate setups where they can be creative with lots of ingredients and other players like a more simple approach, how you handle your setup is up to you. See the [Restaurant Script & Customizations](./) page for general guidelines and rules.

{% hint style="info" %}
All restaurants will select the ingredients that want available at their shop. This helps reduce the number of items that you have to sort through when making a recipe. This ingredient selection will be done as part of your onboarding process.&#x20;
{% endhint %}

#### **Available Ingredients by Category**

{% hint style="success" %}
_Calorie values are listed for reference. The number of calories a final ingredient has determines its final hunger/thirst value for the player consuming your food._ \
\
_Special Effects determine if the final product will make a person drunk, give armor, reduce stress, etc._ \
\
_The final product contains the sum of all ingredients, so if you add a bunch of things that make a person drunk it will make them really drunk._ \
\
_Many ingredients can be sourced in other ways. They do NOT have to be purchased, it is up to you to discover the various ways you_&#x20;
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="danger" %}
Some ingredients must be processed in order to use it and can't be used raw.&#x20;

Some ingredients process into multiple items, for example Raw Beef can be Steak, Ground Beef, etc.

When building your ingredient list please be sure to select items that make sense for your business and based on your approved business plan. For example, Burgershot isn't serving Vodka.
{% endhint %}
