# Transporting

EMS is not required to transport injured players to the hospital if they are reviving those players from a phase 1 injury (on their side). A simple patch up is OK to do on the spot.&#x20;

Transporting to the nearest hospital is required anytime a player is on their back. If there are multiple players in a phase 2 state, then all players must be transported to the hospital and treated at the hospital. If EMS is severely outnumbered they may approve players to self check once at the hospital so they can make another run to pick up more bodies.&#x20;

During a gang war or beef players should be taken to different hospitals to keep the two factions separate&#x20;
