# Perma-Death/Death Penalty

A player may decide to perm-death any of their characters. No civilian player will be forced to perm-death a character, except in the following instances

* The administration team may choose to give your character a Death Penalty if you are considered a repeat offender of deadly crimes or show blatant toxicity with your character’s roleplay.,
* If you are a gang member and you have been informed of their bylaws that by joining the gang you are subject to a perma and under what guidelines that a perma can happen. These bylaws must be told to you when you join the group and must be available to all members in a gang discord, google doc, etc.
* If you are roleplaying a serial killer and elect the death penalty

If a player decides to do a perm-death, they must be declared dead by any on-duty EMS, use the "/me" command to inform the EMS of your intentions when they arrive on the scene. You do not have to report that you are killing off a character to staff on your own.&#x20;

In the event of a self perma you are giving up all property, inventory, cars, and other assets of that character. If you have something purchased with real money (not from a VIP shop where in game money is used) then please open a ticket and we will get that item transferred to a new character. If you are force peram'ed by a gang then as part of the perma process we will review if anything qualifies for transfer.

It should go without saying that regardless of the perma reason all of that characters memories, experiences, relationships (Good and bad) dies with the character. If you create a new character you should seek a new story, new relationships, etc.&#x20;
