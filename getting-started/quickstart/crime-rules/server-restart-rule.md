# Server Restart Rule

## **Server Restart Rules – No Criminal Activity Window**

**The Rule:**

**NO criminal activity is permitted within 30 minutes before OR 30 minutes after a server restart.**

This is a **60-minute total window** (30 minutes on either side of the restart) where criminal RP is off-limits.

***

### **What This Means:**

**30 Minutes BEFORE Restart:**

* No robberies, heists, shootouts, or criminal scenes
* Time to wrap up ongoing RP, store vehicles, and secure belongings
* Focus on low-key, casual roleplay that can end quickly

**30 Minutes AFTER Restart:**

* Allow time for everyone to load in, get situated, and connect properly
* Server stability needs time to settle
* Give players a chance to get their bearings before diving into intense RP

***

### **Why This Rule Exists:**

**1. Server Stability & Performance** Restarts are necessary for server health. Complex criminal scenes with multiple players, vehicles, and mechanics strain the server right when it needs to shut down cleanly.

**2. Fairness & Completion** Criminal RP often takes significant time to play out properly. Starting a bank heist 20 minutes before restart means:

* The scene can't be completed properly
* People lose items, progress, or money due to the restart
* Police/criminals get robbed of a full RP experience
* Disputes arise over what should "count" after restart

**3. Prevents Exploitation** Some players might try to use the restart to their advantage:

* Starting crimes knowing they'll "reset" soon
* Escaping consequences by logging in late after restart
* Using the chaos of restart to avoid capture

**4. Gives Everyone Time To Prepare** Players need time to:

* Store valuable items safely
* Complete transactions and secure money
* Say goodbyes and wrap up casual RP

***

## **What You CANNOT Do During The Window:**

❌ **Any criminal activity, including:**

* Robberies (stores, banks, players, etc.)
* Drug sales or trafficking
* Chop shop activity
* Heists or organized crime
* Shootouts, gang conflicts, or violence
* Kidnappings
* Any illegal activity that requires extended RP or has consequences

❌ **Starting scenes that can't finish:** If there isn't enough time for a full scene to play out realistically before restart – **don't start it.**

**Ask yourself:** "Can this scene reasonably be completed, with all RP and consequences, before the restart?"

If the answer is no – save it for after the window.

***

### **What You CAN Do During The Window:**

✅ **Casual, low-stakes roleplay:**

* Hanging out with friends at a bar, restaurant, or social spot
* Light conversations and relationship building
* Shopping or visiting businesses
* Cruising around the city
* Meeting new people
* Planning future RP or jobs (in-character)
* Wrapping up ongoing legal RP

✅ **Server maintenance tasks:**

* Storing items
* Depositing money in banks
* Organizing inventory
* Securing valuables
* Saying goodbyes before logging off

✅ **Preparation for post-restart:**

* Coordinating with your crew for jobs after restart (in Discord or IC)
* Planning heists or criminal activity to execute once the window closes
* Scouting locations or discussing strategies

**There's plenty of RP to do** – it just needs to be low-key and able to wrap up quickly.

***

### **Time Awareness:**

**Server restarts are announced in advance** – pay attention to:

* In-game announcements
* Discord notifications

**Plan accordingly.** If restart is in 40 minutes, don't start a bank heist that takes an hour to complete.

**Build buffer time into your plans.** Things take longer than expected in RP – assume you need more time than you think.

***

### **What Happens If You Break This Rule:**

**Scenario 1: You start criminal activity too close to restart**

* The scene will likely be interrupted or incomplete
* Any items, money, or consequences may not save properly
* You may lose progress, loot, or face unexpected outcomes
* Staff will not reverse consequences caused by ignoring this rule

**Scenario 2: You intentionally violate the window**

* Disciplinary action for breaking server rules
* No sympathy for lost items/progress – you were warned
* Potential suspension if it's a repeated pattern

**If the restart catches you mid-scene legitimately** (announcements failed, you lost track of time, genuine mistake):

* Do your best to wrap up quickly and safely
* Open a ticket if there are issues with lost items/progress
* Staff will review on a case-by-case basis

**But intentionally ignoring the window? No leniency.**

***

### **Special Circumstances:**

**"But we're in the middle of a chase/shootout when the 30-minute mark hits!"**

If you're already deep in an active scene when the window begins:

* Try to wrap it up as quickly and safely as possible
* Don't drag it out or escalate further
* Accept that the scene might not conclude perfectly
* Both sides should work together to find a reasonable stopping point

**This is why the rule exists** – to prevent these awkward situations. Don't start what you can't finish.

***

### **Enforcement:**

Staff monitors server activity around restarts. If you're consistently pushing the boundaries or ignoring the rule:

* You'll be contacted and warned
* Repeated violations result in disciplinary action
* Using restarts to exploit or grief will result in harsher consequences

**This isn't a suggestion – it's a rule.**

***

### **The Bottom Line:**

**Plan your criminal RP around restart windows, not during them.**

The 30-minute buffer on either side gives everyone time to:

* Finish existing RP properly
* Prepare for the restart
* Load back in smoothly
* Get ready for the next session

**There are 23 hours in a day.** You can spare 60 minutes (30 before, 30 after) to keep the server healthy and fair for everyone.

**Use the downtime wisely** – hang out, build relationships, plan your next move, or just chill. Not every moment needs to be high-stakes action.

_Keep an eye on the clock, respect the restart window, and everyone has a better experience._
