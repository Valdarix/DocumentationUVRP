# Downed & New Life

## **Injury System & Downed States**

PHRP uses a two-phase injury system that emphasizes **realistic recovery and continued storylines** rather than using death mechanics to erase RP. Your character doesn't actually die unless you choose permanent death (perma-death) – they're injured and need time to recover.

**Our philosophy:** Injuries should create ongoing RP opportunities, not end storylines. Just because you're downed doesn't mean the story is over.

{% hint style="danger" %}
The AI doctor exists for convenience and he should only be used when:&#x20;

* No EMS is online
* No Players are able to take you the ER for self check, to an illegal doctor or administer adrenaline.&#x20;

Using the AI doctor should be your last resort as he is the only medical service that requires absolutely zero roleplay. He should not be used as a revive bot just to continue the activity your were doing.&#x20;
{% endhint %}

***

### **Phase 1: Knocked Down / Injured (On Your Side)**

**What This Means:** You've been injured but are still conscious – dazed, hurt, but aware of what's happening around you.

**What You CAN Do:** ✅ Speak in-game (groaning, short responses, calling for help)\
✅ Use your radio to call for assistance\
✅ Communicate with those around you\
✅ Be revived by EMS, an illegal doctor, or given adrenaline by any another player to get back on your feet

**What You CANNOT Do:** ❌ Use your radio if you're cuffed or zip-tied (your hands don't work)\
❌ Act completely fine or uninjured\
❌ Run around immediately after being picked up

**Memory & Recovery:**

* **You remember EVERYTHING** that led to you being knocked down
* You can identify who hurt you and what happened
* **You MUST roleplay being injured for at least 15 minutes** after being picked up (limping, holding injuries, moving slowly, seeking medical attention)

***

### **Phase 2: Severely Injured / Unconscious (On Your Back / Fully Bled Out)**

**What This Means:** You are **severely injured and unconscious**, requiring emergency medical intervention. You've experienced significant trauma that has caused temporary memory loss.

**You are NOT dead** (unless you choose perma-death) – you're critically injured and your body and mind are struggling to process what happened.

***

**Immediate State (While On Your Back):**

**What You CANNOT Do:** ❌ Talk in voice chat\
❌ Use your radio\
❌ Use your phone\
❌ Communicate verbally in any way

**What You CAN Do:** ✅ Communicate with EMS **ONLY** using `/me` commands (describing your condition, groaning, responding to questions non-verbally)

**Why:** You're unconscious and severely injured. Your character cannot speak coherently, use devices, or communicate normally. Act like it.

***

**Revival & Transport:**

**Who Can Revive You:**

* **EMS (Emergency Medical Services)** – they MUST transport you to a hospital
* **Illegal NPC Doctors (Grandma)** – underground medical professionals who can stabilize you
* **Illegal Player Doctors** - If available you may be able to seek out an illegal doctor to save your friend, they may be more expensive as they can set their own price

**Standard players cannot revive you from Phase 2.** You need actual medical intervention.

If EMS revives you, they are **required to transport you to a hospital for treatment**. You cannot decline transport when you're on your back – you're too injured to refuse medical care.

{% hint style="danger" %}
If EMS is active and you are not subject to being arrested you should give EMS a chance to arrive and heal you over all other methods. Using illegal methods should only be used if you need to safely revive without arrest or apprehension.&#x20;
{% endhint %}

***

### **The 1-hour Amnesia Period:**

When you're revived from being on your back, **you have temporary amnesia for 1-hour after being revived.**

**What this means:**

* You don't remember who shot/stabbed/hurt you
* You don't remember the specific details of the confrontation
* You have fragmented, hazy memories of the events leading up to your injury
* It's like waking up confused after a severe concussion

**What you DO remember:**

* Events from earlier in the day (more than 30 minutes before being downed)
* Your relationships, friends, enemies, and ongoing situations
* General context of why you might have been targeted

**Memory Recovery – After 1 Hour:** After **1 real-life hour** has passed since you were downed, **your memory returns and you can fully remember the incident.**

* The fog clears
* You remember who attacked you
* You remember what was said and what happened
* You can now act on that information

**Unlike other servers, you are NOT required to completely forget forever.** Your character experienced trauma, but their memory eventually returns.

***

#### **The 1-Hour Mandatory Recovery Period:**

**After being revived from Phase 2, you MUST spend at least 1 hour recovering from your injuries.**

**What this looks like:**

✅ **Immediate aftermath (first 5 minutes):**

* EMS may place you on crutches for immediate RP purposes
* You're weak, disoriented, in pain
* You need help getting around

✅ **Recovery period (30 Minutes):**

* Rest at a hospital, home, or safe location
* Roleplay the healing process (changing bandages, taking medication, sleeping)
* Move slowly, favor injuries, show signs of trauma
* Seek follow-up medical care if appropriate
* Process what happened emotionally and mentally

After 1 hour your memory is back and you are fully back to normal.

❌ **What you CANNOT do during recovery:**

* Jump right back into another dangerous scene
* Immediately return to criminal activity
* Act completely fine as if nothing happened
* Engage in physically demanding activities
* Put yourself in situations where you could be reinjured immediately

**Why this matters:** You were just critically injured. Your body needs time to heal. Jumping back into action immediately is unrealistic and breaks immersion.

***

### **Retaliation & Revenge:**

**Just because you remember who hurt you doesn't mean you can instantly retaliate.**

❌ **NOT Allowed:**

* Getting your memory back after 1 hour and immediately going after the person who downed you
* Seeking revenge the same day you were critically injured
* Acting like the injury didn't happen once you remember

✅ **Realistic Approach:**

* Take time to physically AND mentally recover
* Plan your response carefully over days
* Consider whether your character would even want revenge or if they'd be traumatized
* Build tension and create a better story through patience

**Think about it:** Someone just put you in the hospital. You're hurt, possibly scared, definitely weakened. Why would you immediately go back for round two?

**Great RP comes from the slow burn** – recovering, planning, building alliances, and striking back when it makes sense for your character.

***

### **EMS Neutrality & Information:**

**EMS has federal protection and must remain neutral.**

When you wake up and ask EMS what happened:

✅ **EMS CAN tell you:**

* What medical procedures they performed
* The nature of your injuries (gunshot wounds, stab wounds, blunt trauma, etc.)
* Where you were found
* Your current medical condition

❌ **EMS CANNOT tell you:**

* Who brought you to them
* Who shot/stabbed/hurt you (even if they know)
* Details about the incident that would compromise their neutrality
* Information that would give you an unfair advantage

**EMS exists to save lives, not to feed you information for retaliation.**

If someone who was involved in the scene wants to tell you what happened, they can – but EMS stays out of it.

***

### **Memory Restoration By Others:**

**People who were present during the incident can help restore your memory** once you're conscious and recovering.

✅ **Allowed:**

* Friends/crew members reminding you what happened
* Witnesses sharing their perspective
* Being shown evidence (photos, videos, items from the scene)

**However:** This should happen naturally through RP, not immediately as you wake up. Give your recovery period its due time.

***

### **Why This System Exists:**

**1. More Realistic RP** Real injuries have lasting consequences. People don't instantly bounce back from being shot or stabbed. This system respects that reality.

**2. Continued Storylines** Memory loss mechanics on other servers often end story arcs abruptly and unrealistically. Our system allows conflicts to continue and evolve naturally.

**3. Prevents "Backing" To End RP** Some players use the death/memory-wipe mechanic to escape consequences or end storylines they don't like. By allowing memory recovery, we prevent this abuse.

**4. You'd Find Out Anyway** Let's be honest – even with full memory wipes, players always find out who attacked them through friends, witnesses, or OOC information. Our system acknowledges this reality and builds it into the rules.

**5. Creates Better Stories** Injuries, recovery, trauma, and eventual confrontation create richer, more layered narratives than instant respawns with no memory.

***

### **Special Circumstances:**

**Perma-Death (Permanent Character Death):** If you **choose** to permanently kill your character, that's your decision and the memory rules don't apply – your character is gone.

**Respawning Without EMS:** If you respawn at a hospital without EMS assistance (bleeding out completely), the same rules apply – you're severely injured with temporary amnesia, and you must follow the 1-hour recovery period.

***

### **Summary / Quick Reference:**

**Phase 1 (On Your Side):**

* Can talk and use radio (unless restrained)
* Remember everything immediately
* Must act injured for 15 minutes
* Can be revived by EMS, illegal doctors, or adrenaline

**Phase 2 (On Your Back):**

* Cannot talk, use radio, or use phone
* Can only communicate with EMS via /me
* Memory fully returns after 1 hour
* MUST recover for at least 1 hour (no jumping back into action)
* Can only be revived by EMS (with hospital transport) or illegal doctors
* Cannot immediately retaliate even after memory returns

***

### **The Bottom Line:**

Injuries matter. Recovery takes time. Memories return. Stories continue.

**Treat your character's injuries with the gravity they deserve.** Being shot isn't a minor inconvenience – it's a traumatic, life-altering event that should shape your character's decisions and story.

**Take the time to recover, process, and plan.** The best revenge is served cold, and the best stories are built on patience and realism.
