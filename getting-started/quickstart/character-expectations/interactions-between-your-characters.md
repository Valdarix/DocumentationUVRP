# Interactions between your characters

## **Multiple Characters (Alts) – Rules & Separation**

**You ARE allowed to have multiple characters** in the city. Playing different characters can be a great way to explore diverse storylines, perspectives, and roleplay experiences.

**However, your characters must remain completely separate.** They exist in the same world but should never overlap in ways that benefit you as a player.

***

### **Character Separation – The Core Rules:**

**1. Your Characters Cannot Know Each Other**

From an in-character perspective, your characters have never met and do not know of each other's existence.

❌ **Not Allowed:**

* Mentioning your other character in conversation in any way.&#x20;
* Having your characters work together or interact in any way even through third party characters
* Using knowledge from one character to benefit another

✅ **Allowed:**

* Your characters existing in the same city without interaction
* Both characters being part of different friend groups, businesses, or storylines

**They're strangers. Keep them that way.**

{% hint style="warning" %}
They are not cousins, souls, spirits, etc. They are completely different characters with different motivations, relationships and backgrounds.&#x20;

Your characters have never met, so refrain from phrases like my cousin, or on my other soul.&#x20;
{% endhint %}

***

**2. No Asset Transfers Between Characters**

You **cannot transfer money, items, vehicles, properties, or any other assets** between your characters – directly or indirectly.

❌ **Explicitly Prohibited:**

* Giving money from Character A to Character B in any way
* Buying items on one character to give to your other character
* Dropping items/weapons for your alt to pick up
* **Using a third-party "friend" to transfer assets between your characters** (this is still a violation even if someone else facilitates it)
* Sharing vehicles, properties, or businesses between your alts
* Any creative workaround to funnel resources from one character to another

**Why this matters:** This is essentially exploiting the system to double your wealth and resources. Each character should earn their own way independently.

**Consequences:**

* Transferred items/money will be deleted from your inventory
* Disciplinary action up to and including character wipes or bans

***

**3. No Shared Relationships Across Characters**

Your alternative characters should not have the same relationships, friend groups, or connections with other players.

❌ **Not Allowed:**

* All your characters being friends with the same group of people
* Both characters working for the same business or gang
* Recreating the exact same social circle on every character (you can have the same group OOC but not the same IC group)

✅ **Allowed:**

* Naturally developing different relationships on each character
* Your characters being in completely different social spheres

**Why this matters:** Your alts should provide diverse experiences, not duplicate the same relationships with different character names. Explore new dynamics, make new friends, and create unique stories for each character.

**The exception:** If it happens organically and makes sense (e.g., both characters visit the same popular business), that's fine – but don't force it or recreate identical social structures.

***

### **Cop & Criminal Alts – Allowed But Strictly Separated**

**You ARE allowed to have both a Law Enforcement character and a criminal/gang member character.**

This can provide great perspective and diverse roleplay experiences. However, the separation rules are even more critical here.

**Absolute Requirements:**

**Your characters do NOT know each other and NEVER share knowledge.**

❌ **Critical Violations:**

* Your cop character knows about a planned raid on your gang → **Your gang member character does NOT know this and cannot warn anyone**
* Your criminal character learns about a rival gangs drug operation → **Your cop character doesn't suddenly "figure it out"**
* Using knowledge from one character to benefit the other in any way
* Avoiding situations where your characters would interact (e.g., your cop "conveniently" not being online during your gang's crime)

✅ **What IS Allowed:**

* Your cop arresting your criminal friends - treat them as strangers
* Experiencing both sides of law enforcement RP authentically and separately

***

#### **The Consequence For Violating Cop/Criminal Separation:**

If you fail to keep your Law Enforcement and criminal characters completely separate – **you will be forced to choose one or the other.**

You cannot play both if you can't maintain the integrity of each role. This protects the server's balance and ensures fair play for everyone.

**Pattern Recognition:**

* One questionable moment? We'll investigate and provide guidance.
* Multiple incidents of overlap, meta-gaming, or shared knowledge? You'll be forced to pick one character and delete the other.

***

**Why These Rules Exist:**

Multiple characters are a privilege, not a right. They're meant to:

* Let you explore different playstyles and storylines
* Provide fresh perspectives and experiences
* Prevent burnout by switching between character types

**They are NOT meant to:**

* Double your resources or wealth
* Give you insider information from multiple angles
* Allow you to "win" by exploiting multiple character knowledge

**Each character should stand on their own** - their own money, their own storylines, and their own knowledge.

***

**The Golden Rule:**

**If you're benefiting as a player from having multiple characters (beyond just having variety in RP), you're probably breaking the rules.**

Your characters should be completely independent people living separate lives. The moment they start helping each other, sharing knowledge, or overlapping in ways that benefit you – that's a problem.

_Questions about whether something crosses the line with your alts? Open a ticket and ask before doing it._
