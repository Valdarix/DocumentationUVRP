# 🎯 Hitman RP

## **Hitman & Bounty RP – Allowed With Requirements**

Hitman and bounty roleplay adds intrigue, danger, and consequences to the criminal underworld – **but it must be done thoughtfully to avoid becoming toxic or immersion-breaking.**

When done right, this creates compelling storylines. When done poorly, it becomes a way to avoid real RP and grief other players.

{% hint style="danger" %}
The server only allows TWO(2) hitmen to be active in the city at any given time. Becoming a hitman requires that you open a roleplay ticket in the Discord and explain your idea for being a hitman. Upon approval you can begin roleplaying as a hitman. \
\
Bounties do not require any special approval.&#x20;
{% endhint %}

***

### **Core Requirements For ALL Hits & Bounties:**

**1. Sufficient Roleplay Justification**

You cannot put a hit or bounty on someone just because you don't like them or had a minor disagreement.

✅ **Valid Reasons:**

* Betrayal or snitching to authorities
* Serious disrespect or threats against you/your organization
* Ongoing conflict that has escalated significantly
* Business disputes with major consequences
* Revenge for serious harm done to you or your crew
* Breaking significant agreements or codes

❌ **NOT Valid Reasons:**

* Someone insulted you once
* A minor argument or disagreement
* You lost a fair fight and want revenge
* Personal OOC dislike disguised as IC conflict
* Petty grievances with no real weight

**The standard:** Would a reasonable person in the criminal world actually spend money to have this person killed or captured over this issue?

***

**2. The 24-Hour Public Knowledge Rule**

**Before anyone can collect a bounty or a hitman can strike, the hit/bounty must be PUBLIC KNOWLEDGE for at least 24 hours.**

**What This Means:**

* The target (and the community) must be made aware that there's a price on their head
* This can be done through word of mouth, social media posts, in game systems or direct warnings
* The information must actually spread – you can't just whisper it to one person and call it "public"
* The 24-hour timer starts when it becomes reasonably known in the criminal community

**Why this rule exists:**

* Gives the target time to react, prepare, or negotiate
* Creates RP opportunities (people seeking protection, making deals, hiring security)
* Prevents cheap, surprise kills with no interaction
* Allows the tension and drama to build naturally

**The target should know they're being hunted** – that's where the best stories come from.

{% hint style="danger" %}
Once a hit or bounty has been completed it must be made known that the contract has already been executed.&#x20;
{% endhint %}

***

**Bounties vs. Hits – Know The Difference**

**BOUNTY:**

* **Objective:** Capture the target **alive** and deliver them to the person who placed the bounty
* **Open to anyone:** Any player can attempt to collect
* **Payment:** Made upon successful delivery of the living target
* **RP Outcome:** Creates interrogation scenes, negotiations, possible release or execution

**Example:** "I'm offering $50,000 to anyone who brings me John Doe alive. He has information I need."

{% hint style="danger" %}
You should only try to collect a bounty if it makes sense for you character. If you are a respectable business owner does it make sense to drop everything and go hunting a bounty?
{% endhint %}

***

**HIT:**

* **Objective:** Kill the target
* **Exclusive contract:** You must find and hire a specific hitman (not open to everyone)
* **Payment:** Negotiated between the person placing the hit and the hitman
* **RP Outcome:** Assassination, potential investigation, blowback, etc.

**Example:** "I need someone taken care of permanently. Can you handle it?" (Private negotiation with a hitman)

***

### **Playing A Hitman – Do It Right**

Hitmen can be compelling characters, but they require thoughtful, realistic roleplay.

#### **How To Play A Hitman Properly:**

✅ **Be secretive and discreet**

* Operate in the shadows
* Build a reputation through word-of-mouth, not advertisements
* Use coded language and private meetings
* Maintain a cover identity or legitimate business front

✅ **Be selective about contracts**

* Don't accept every hit thrown your way
* Have standards (won't kill cops, only takes serious contracts, etc. You decide but you should have some)
* Negotiate terms and payment realistically
* Consider whether the hit makes sense

✅ **Operate professionally**

* Research your target
* Plan the hit carefully
* Execute cleanly (once the 24-hour period has passed)
* Leave minimal evidence

✅ **Have depth beyond "hitman"**

* This cannot be your entire character concept
* Have relationships, hobbies, a cover story
* Be part of the broader criminal world (maybe you're in a gang, run a business, etc.)
* Show personality beyond just being a killer

❌ **What NOT To Do:**

* Openly advertising "hitman services" in public or on social media
* Acting like a cartoon assassin with no subtlety
* Making your entire identity "professional killer" with no other traits
* Taking every contract with no standards or selectivity
* Operating with no concern for consequences or investigation

#### **Special Kill Permissions For Hitmen**

**Once the 24-hour public knowledge period has passed**, a hitman **MAY** kill their target without direct interaction in that moment.

**What this means:**

* The hitman can ambush, snipe, or eliminate the target without a conversation immediately before the kill
* The "RP before violence" requirement is fulfilled by the contract, buildup, and 24-hour warning period
* The target should have had time to prepare, hire protection, or negotiate

**However:**

* The hit still needs to make sense (don't kill them in the middle of a police station)
* There should still be _some_ RP around it (the planning, the approach, the aftermath)
* If possible, brief interaction adds to the scene ("John Doe sends his regards")

**This is NOT a license to:**

* RDM with a flimsy "I was hired" excuse
* Skip all RP and just shoot on sight constantly
* Ignore the 24-hour rule
* Kill with zero buildup or justification

### **Consequences & Blowback**

**Being a hitman comes with risks:**

✅ Your activities can cause blowback to your organization

* If you're in a gang and doing hits, those contracts could bring heat to your crew
* Rival gangs may retaliate against your associates
* Police investigations could expose your connections

✅ Your reputation follows you

* If you're known as a killer, people treat you differently
* You may become a target yourself
* Law enforcement may investigate you

✅ Failed hits have consequences

* If you fail to complete a contract, your reputation suffers
* The target now knows you're coming
* You may face retaliation

**This isn't consequence-free murder** – play the character realistically and accept the outcomes.

### **Preventing Toxic Behavior**

These rules exist to prevent hitman/bounty RP from becoming toxic:

❌ **Not Allowed:**

* Using hits as a lazy way to avoid direct conflict RP
* Placing bounties on people you have OOC issues with
* Constantly having hits out on the same players to harass them
* Operating as a hitman with no depth, just grinding kills
* Ignoring the 24-hour rule to get cheap kills

✅ **Encouraged:**

* Using hits/bounties as climactic moments in long-term storylines
* Creating tension and drama through the 24-hour buildup period
* Playing hitmen as complex characters with motivations beyond money
* Allowing targets opportunities to negotiate, escape, or turn the tables

### **The Bottom Line:**

Hitman and bounty RP can create incredible stories – shadowy negotiations, tense cat-and-mouse games, dramatic assassinations, and complex criminal politics.

**But it requires:**

* Strong RP justification
* 24-hour public knowledge period
* Realistic, nuanced character work (especially for hitmen)
* Respect for the target's ability to react and create counter-RP

**Use this RP tool wisely. Make it meaningful, not toxic.**
