# ❌ Offensive RP

## **Prohibited Content**

Certain topics are off-limits in PHRP, regardless of context or intent. These rules exist to protect our community and ensure everyone feels safe while playing.

### **Sexual Assault Roleplay – Absolute Zero Tolerance**

**Strictly Prohibited:** Roleplaying any form of sexual assault, including but not limited to:

* Rape or attempted rape
* Non-consensual touching or groping through animations, emotes or `/me`
* Sexual coercion or manipulation
* Any scenario involving sexual violence

**This rule has NO exceptions:**

* ✗ Not allowed even with consent from all parties involved
* ✗ Not allowed "off-screen" or implied
* ✗ Not allowed as part of a character's backstory being actively roleplayed
* ✗ Not allowed under any circumstances

**Consequences:** Immediate permanent ban with **no possibility of appeal**

***

### **Suicide Roleplay – Not Allowed**

**Strictly Prohibited:** Roleplaying suicide, suicide attempts, or suicidal ideation in any form.

**Why this rule exists:** Suicide is a serious real-world issue that affects many people. Roleplaying it can be deeply triggering for community members and trivializes a tragic reality. We're here to create entertaining stories, not to explore or depict self-harm.

**This includes:**

* Active suicide attempts or completions
* Threatening suicide as a manipulation tactic in RP
* Detailed discussions or planning of suicide in character
* Using suicide as a plot device or storyline

**Consequences:** Disciplinary action up to and including permanent ban.

**If you're struggling:** If you or someone you know is experiencing suicidal thoughts, please reach out to crisis resources like the National Suicide Prevention Lifeline (**988** in the US) or contact a trusted person for support.

***

### **Mental Illness Roleplay – Not Allowed**

**Strictly Prohibited:** Roleplaying characters with mental illnesses or using mental health conditions as character traits.

**Why this rule exists:** Mental illness is a serious, real-world medical condition that affects millions of people. Roleplaying it often leads to:

* Harmful stereotypes and misrepresentation
* Trivializing genuine struggles people face
* Using mental illness as an excuse for poor behavior or rule-breaking
* Creating uncomfortable or triggering scenarios for community members

**This includes:**

* Depression, anxiety disorders, bipolar disorder, schizophrenia, PTSD, etc.
* Using mental illness as justification for your character's actions
* Roleplaying "insanity" or "crazy" characters based on mental health stereotypes

**What you CAN do:**

* Roleplay complex, flawed characters with realistic emotions and reactions
* Have your character experience sadness, anger, fear, or stress in response to situations
* Create characters with personality quirks, unique perspectives, or interesting backstories

**The difference:** Human emotion and response vs. clinical mental illness. Your character can be sad about something that happened – they can't have clinical depression.

**Consequences:** Disciplinary action based on severity.

### **When In Doubt:**

If you're unsure whether a topic or scenario crosses the line, **don't do it.** Open a ticket and ask staff first. We'd rather clarify boundaries than deal with violations.

**Our community is built on respect, safety, and immersive roleplay.** These rules help us maintain that foundation.
