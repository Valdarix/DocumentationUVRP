# Lurking

### **Activity Requirements & Timeline:**

We're building an active, engaged community – and that means we need members who are genuinely here to participate. Here's what we expect:

**After Joining Discord:** You have **72 hours** to review the rulebook, read through the player guide, and submit your whitelist application. If we don't receive your application within this timeframe, you'll be removed from the community.

**After Whitelist Approval:** Getting approved is just the beginning! You have **7 days** to log into the city for the first time. Lurking in Discord without actually playing isn't what we're about – we want active players, not spectators.

**Ongoing Activity:** We monitor playtime to keep our community vibrant. If you've been inactive for an extended period, we'll open a ticket to check in with you. You'll have **72 hours to respond**. No response means we'll assume you've moved on, and you'll be removed from the city with your character(s) deleted.

***

### **Why These Requirements?**

This isn't about being harsh or punishing people for taking breaks – we get that life happens. Here's what we're actually trying to accomplish:

✅ **Keep our database manageable** – prevents technical bloat and performance issues\
✅ **Reduce trolling and drama** – inactive accounts attract problems\
✅ **Maintain an active player base** – ensures everyone has people to roleplay with\
✅ **Better slot management** – knowing our active player count means no priority queues needed

**Taking a break?** Just let us know! Open a ticket before you go inactive, and we'll work with you. Communication goes a long way.
