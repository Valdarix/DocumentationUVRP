# 🎧 Third-party Communication is Allowed

**You ARE allowed** to be in private Discord calls, phone calls, or any other external communication while playing in the city. We're not here to police how you stay connected with your friends.

**However – and this is crucial:**

All decisions about your character and their actions **must be made and roleplayed in-city.** Your character can only know what they've experienced, seen, or been told through roleplay. If you're not vocalizing your character's thoughts, plans, or actions within the game – especially when interacting with others – that's meta-gaming.

**In simple terms:** External calls are for OOC (out-of-character) chat. In-city voice is for IC (in-character) roleplay. Keep them separate.

***

**Why We Allow External Communication:**

We're being realistic and transparent here:

1. **You're going to do it anyway** – we'd rather acknowledge it and set clear expectations than pretend it doesn't happen.
2. **It actually helps with roleplay** – having a Discord call for OOC banter means you can keep all your out-of-character conversations separate from your in-city roleplay. No more breaking character to say "brb" or "lol that was crazy" in-game.

**The key:** Use external comms to _enhance_ your roleplay by keeping OOC out of the city, not to gain unfair information or skip important roleplay moments.

_Questions about what counts as meta-gaming? Staff is always here to help clarify!_
