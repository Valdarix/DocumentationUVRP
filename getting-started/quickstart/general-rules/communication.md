# 🇺🇲 Communication

### **Language Requirements:**&#x20;

We're a North American-based community, so all members need to be able to communicate comfortably in English (understanding, speaking, and typing).

### **Voice Communication:**&#x20;

A working microphone is required for roleplay. That said, we're committed to being an inclusive community for everyone, including those with disabilities. If you have a disability that makes using a microphone difficult or impossible, please reach out to any staff member – we'll gladly work with you to find an alternative communication solution.

### Voice Changers:

You may use a voice changer in city, but only to alter your voice in a believable way. Voice changers that create an unrealistic voice effect are NOT allowed. Comedy RP can be achieved without the crutch of grating and annoying cartoon voices.&#x20;

### Push To Talk:

All players must have Push To Talk enabled at all times.
