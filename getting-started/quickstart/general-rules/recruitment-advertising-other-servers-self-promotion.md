# Recruitment/Advertising Other Servers/Self Promotion

We've built this community with care, and we ask that you respect it by keeping recruitment and advertising off our platforms.

**What's Not Allowed:**

* Advertising, promoting, or recruiting for other servers (in any PHRP Discord channel, voice chat, or in-game)
* Self-promotion of your own communities or projects
* Sending unsolicited Direct Messages or invite links to our members
* Using your **PHRP Discord server profile** "About Me" section to advertise other communities.

**Important Note:** Your main Discord account profile can say whatever you'd like – we only ask that your profile specifically within PHRP Discord servers doesn't advertise other communities.

**Why this rule?** We're all here to build something special together. This isn't about gatekeeping – it's about respecting the space we're creating and the people in it.

_Questions about what's appropriate? Reach out to staff – we're happy to clarify!_

{% hint style="warning" %}
Discord Badges - Everyone loves the Discord Badge/Tags beside their username. We won't ask you to change your tag because it is not server profile specific. However if you have Discord Nitro you must setup a PHRP Profile with an About Me, Avatar, and Banner that DO NOT advertise other cities. \
\
If you do not have Nitro you may be asked to change your Discord Profile if it advertises another city.&#x20;
{% endhint %}
