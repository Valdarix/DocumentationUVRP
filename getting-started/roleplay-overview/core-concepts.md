# Core Concepts

1. Your character is not you.
2. If you have multiple characters, they don't know each other, they have never met and they should not share knowledge with other people that they should not know. For example, if your cop character knows your gang is about to get raided you are not allowed to warn your gang.&#x20;
3. You cannot identify someone by their voice, especially across multiple characters.&#x20;
4. You should not treat someone's alternate character a certain way because of issues you have with another one of their characters.&#x20;
5. Stay in RP, if you find yourself becoming heated in a scene, find a way out of the scene using RP. If you find that participating in RP is causing you stress or interrupting your single player experience, then you should evaluate your participation in an RP server.&#x20;
