# Who are we and what are we about?

### **Our Story – Finding Home**

Point Hope RP was born from a simple desire: to find a place that truly felt like home.

Our founders spent years bouncing between servers, searching for a community that valued **storytelling over shootouts, character depth over quick content, and genuine connections over grinding.** Time and time again, they found communities that fell short – places where players were treated as revenue streams rather than valued members, where drama overshadowed roleplay, or where "winning" mattered more than creating memorable stories.

**So we built what we couldn't find.**

Point Hope RP is the server we always wished existed – a place where every player can feel safe, respected, and valued. A place where your story matters more than your bank account, and where the journey means more than the destination.

**Welcome home.**

***

### **Who We Are**

Our team is built from **like-minded individuals who genuinely love roleplay.** We're not here to create another clone server or chase trends – we're here to build something meaningful.

**Our Owner:**

* Professional software developer with over 20 years of experience in enterprise software design
* Hobbyist game developer outside of FiveM
* Passionate about creating systems that empower players to tell their stories

**Our Team:** A diverse group united by one core belief: **roleplay and storytelling come first.** We're writers, gamers, creators, and community builders who believe that the best moments in FiveM come from genuine character interactions, not body counts.

***

### **Our Core Values**

#### **Roleplay Over Gunplay**

We value character development, storytelling, and meaningful interactions over constant violence. Guns have their place in our world, but they're tools for storytelling – not the whole story.

#### **Quality Over Quantity**

We'd rather have 50 engaged, creative roleplayers than 500 players grinding and shooting. We're building a community, not a content farm.

#### **Fair Treatment for Everyone**

You're not a walking wallet to us. You're a valued member of our community. Whether you're VIP or free-to-play, you deserve respect, fairness, and equal access to great roleplay.

#### **Transparency & Respect**

We believe in open communication with our community. Your voice matters. Your feedback matters. And we'll always be honest with you about our decisions and direction.

***

### **What Makes Us Different**

#### **Quality Scripts & Custom Development**

Yes, we have all the usual suspects – heists, weapons, extensive clothing options, and **hundreds of addon vehicles with custom engine sounds.** But we don't just throw every script we find at the server and hope it sticks. We also focus on making sure there is something for everyone. Detailed business RP? Check! Fun things to do that are not crimes? Check!

**Our approach:**

* We only add the **best, most polished scripts** available
* When existing scripts don't meet our standards, **we enhance them**
* When nothing exists to fit our vision, **we build it ourselves**
* Everything is optimized for performance and integrated seamlessly

**We're not interested in quantity – we're obsessed with quality.**

***

#### **In-City Roleplay Systems**

We believe that as much as possible should happen **in the city, through roleplay** – not through Discord tickets, applications, or out-of-character bureaucracy.

**Example: Our Unique Gang System**

Gang creation, approval, territory control, and even gang discipline are **entirely in-city roleplay elements.** No applications. No tickets. No purchases.

* Want to start a gang? Build it through RP.
* Want territory? Earn it through RP.
* Facing consequences? Experience them through RP.

**The city is alive, and your actions shape it** – not forms and admin menus.

***

#### **Fair & Transparent Monetization**

Let's be real: **running a quality FiveM server is expensive.** We have to monetize somehow to keep the lights on and continue improving.

**But we do it differently.**

#### **Our VIP & Tebex Philosophy:**

**VIP and Tebex purchases are your way of supporting us** because you like what we do and want to see us continue to grow – **not because you need to unlock features to enjoy the game.**

**What VIP Gets You:** ✅ Exclusive MLO housing options\
✅ Discounts on non-MLO housing\
✅ Access to dealerships with addon vehicles\
✅ Supporting the server's growth and development as part of our steering committee.

**What's Available to EVERYONE (Free Players Included):** ✅ Basic housing options\
✅ Access to addon cars (through different means)\
✅ Full access to all gameplay systems\
✅ Equal opportunity for great roleplay

**We are NOT pay-to-win.** VIP gives you convenience and aesthetic options, not advantages. A free player can have just as rich and fulfilling an experience as a VIP member.

***

#### **Your Investments Are Safe**

Here's something that sets us apart: **We never wipe players to release version upgrades just to make you pay again.**

Some servers wipe everything whenever they do a major update, forcing you to re-purchase vehicles, properties, and everything else. **We don't do that.**

**When you invest in PHRP – whether it's time, money, or both – you can feel safe knowing:**

* Your purchases are permanent
* Your progress won't be arbitrarily erased
* Your investment is respected

**Anything you earn or buy in Point Hope RP will be available to you for as long as you're part of our community.**

***

### **Our Vision for the Future**

Point Hope RP isn't just a server – it's a **growing community** built on shared values.

We're constantly:

* Listening to player feedback
* Improving existing systems
* Adding quality content (never just for the sake of adding)
* Building storylines and events that involve the whole city
* Creating opportunities for players to leave their mark

**We're not chasing player counts or viral clips.** We're building something that lasts – a place where people want to stay, not just visit.
