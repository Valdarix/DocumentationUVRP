# Getting Started

### **Character Creation (Name and Vitals)**

{% hint style="info" %}
Selecting mp\_m\_freemode\_1 or mp\_f\_freemode\_1 will give you a fully customizable character. Selecting any other ped from the drop-down list will severely limit the customization options available to your character.&#x20;
{% endhint %}

Have fun creating your character, and take your time, consider the information in the [roleplay guide](../../getting-started/roleplay-overview/character-creation-and-development.md) and make sure your character appearance fits the character you are making. Once you are finished press the save icon in the appearance menu to begin your journey.&#x20;

{% hint style="info" %}
Our clothing menu is setup to hide clothing that you can't wear or clothing that is broken for some reason so you may notice that numbers are skipped when you go through the menus. This is intended and not a bug.&#x20;
{% endhint %}
