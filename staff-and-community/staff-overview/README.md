# Staff Overview

No FiveM city can thrive without a dedicated volunteer staff and Point Hope RP is no different. Our player first staff is here to make sure that everyone has an enjoyable time in city, but also to help make sure that things are running smoothly.&#x20;

{% hint style="success" %}
Staff in PHRP are held to a high standard. They exist to keep things running smoothly and should always put RP first.
{% endhint %}

***

The Point Hope RP staff guide is publicly available for all to review in order to provide transparency to all members of the community. [https://cornerstonedev.gitbook.io/staffguide](https://cornerstonedev.gitbook.io/staffguide)

All regularly scheduled staff meetings are hosted in the Discord and all community members are welcome to attend the stream to listen in but community members will not participate directly in the meetings.&#x20;

{% hint style="info" %}
Staff Applications are open periodically when the need arises.&#x20;
{% endhint %}
