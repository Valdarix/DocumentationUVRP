# Meetings

## Community Meetings

* Community meetings will take place every 4-6 weeks.
* These meetings will have an agenda that is posted at least 1 week prior.&#x20;
* There will be a thread where you can post your questions and comments about the upcoming meeting and from that thread we will add onto the agenda.&#x20;
* During the meeting staff will present the agenda and then call up select people from the community to present items from the thread.&#x20;
* Meetings will be scheduled to last 30-45 minutes and will always start on time. Items that do not get discussed from the agenda will be moved to the next meeting.&#x20;
* They will be broadcast on Twitch for those who cannot get on Discord at that time but can access a stream and to provide a VOD of the meeting.&#x20;

## Staff Meetings

* Will take place every 2-4 weeks depending on need.&#x20;
* Will be live broadcast Discord for members of the community to watch and listen to and to keep a VOD record of the meeting.&#x20;
* These will have an agenda and will last no more than 1 hour.&#x20;
