# Discord

The Point Hope RP Discord is the hub of the city. We use the Discord for all out of city communications. This includes

* Whitelist applications
* Staff applications
* Department applications
* Support Tickets
* Development Changelogs
* City Event Planning
* and so much more.&#x20;

We hope that you can find a home in our community, and we look forward to growing with you!\\

**IMPORTANT: We ask that you change your Discord profile for the PHRP Discord to your in-city name(s), this helps everyone know who is talking to who.**&#x20;
